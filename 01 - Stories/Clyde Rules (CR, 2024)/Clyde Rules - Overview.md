# Clyde Rules

## Premise
The story tells the happenings around twelve year old, who struggles with ADHD and dyslexia while also hallucinating the dead outlaw, who is the entire reason for what happens in the story. Jamie gets more and more reckless and trust lesser in authority figures if they're not Clyde, which ultimately leads to his death.
