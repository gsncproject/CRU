# Clyde Rules

## Premise
The story follows twelve-year-old [[Jamie Taylor]], who struggles with ADHD and dyslexia while hallucinating/encountering [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]], a supernatural entity who becomes the fatherly figure Jamie desperately needs after his father, [[James Taylor Senior]], died when Jamie was young. Clyde manipulates Jamie, who increasingly trusts him over authority figures. [[Detective Richard Thornton]] tries to stop Jamie, but Jamie eventually attempts to kill Richard, who kills Jamie and then hides the evidence of what he did.

## Plot Summary
Jamie becomes increasingly attached to Clyde and believes Clyde over other authority figures. Clyde exploits Jamie's vulnerability and need for a fatherly figure. Jamie's escalating behavior eventually leads to a confrontation with Detective Richard Thornton. Jamie attempts to kill Richard, and Richard kills Jamie. Richard conceals the evidence, creating the mystery investigated in [[The Jamie Taylor Case - Overview|The Jamie Taylor Case]].

## Themes

- Grief and the need for parental figures
- Trust and authority
- Childhood vulnerability
- Death and secrecy
