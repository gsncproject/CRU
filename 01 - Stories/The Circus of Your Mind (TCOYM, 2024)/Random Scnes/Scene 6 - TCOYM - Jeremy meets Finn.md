**Characters in this scene**: [[Jeremy Cade]], [[Korra Thornton]], [[Finn Kaelen]], Loenna Thornton (mentioned)

It isn‘t that uncommon that Korra invites me over, but it certainly is uncommon when she invites me over ‘to talk‘. I can assure you that if someone ever tells you they just want to talk, there is something about to be very wrong.

This time my feeling wasn‘t wrong for once. Which is quite a miracle actually.

As always I let myself in with the spare key to her flat. Loenna really hates when I let myself in, but since it‘s Korra‘s flat, that gremlin has nothing to say. 

I barely stand in the door when Korra shouts, „In the kitchen, Jer!“

„Coming…“ I shut the door behind me, and something felt deeply wrong. Like… like Loenna set something on fire and blamed me for it.

I make my way to the kitchen and stop when I see what‘s happening in there. A guy, I have never seen before, sits with Korra at the table and Korra is smiling like someone had just told her the New York Times is offering her a job.

„Erm…“ I manage, what else do you say? There is a stranger in my best friend aka crush‘s kitchen.

The stranger stands up and offers me his hand, „Hi, I‘m Finn.“

Stupid name. I don‘t like him. I don‘t like the name Finn. Finn should be burnt at the stake. 

Slowly I accept it and shake his hand back, „Jeremy.“

„I know.“

You know? YOU know? Helpless I glance at Korra, who is still smiling and slowly I start to realize what is actually going on. This is bad. This is worst than bad.

Finn nervously scratches his neck, „So, Jeremy,“ he starts, and its not even a good start for that conversation we‘re about to have. Death is better than whatever that lad is going to say, „Korra?“ Finn looks at her for backup.

She looks caught off guard. Help me. This is worse than worst. „Korra?“ I repeat slowly.

„So!“ she chirps, „Finn and I…“ 

God. Please don‘t say it.

Korra‘s eyes wander between Finn and I, „Jer, you know how you and I know each other for 23 years? And you know, that I cannot— that I don‘t want to have secrets from you?“

„Yeah?“ I look at her. She should just do it like ripping off a band aid. I‘ll die either way.

„Finn and I are dating,“ she blurts out and Finn just smiles stupid. I hate that guy.

For a moment my brain stops. I know she was going to say it, I knew it. But hearing it? Is it still the wrong time to tell her I‘ve been in love with her since year six? 

The smile I put on feels fake, it probably looks fake too, „Happy for you… super… happy.“ I‘m absolutely not happy. I can feel my heart shattering because I… it‘s no use.

Finn looks fake-concerned, „You sure, mate? S-Sure this is all fine?“

I nod, keeping that ugly smile on my face.

„I know you and Korra are close friends and—„

I shake my head, „Nah, it‘s fine,“ my smile drops, „Just go fuck yourself.“ Then I storm off. I hear Korra yelling my name, but I barely hear it.

Shit. Shit. Shit! Over fifteen years and I still didn‘t have the courage to confess to her and now she‘s dating some fuck up. Great job, Jeremy!
