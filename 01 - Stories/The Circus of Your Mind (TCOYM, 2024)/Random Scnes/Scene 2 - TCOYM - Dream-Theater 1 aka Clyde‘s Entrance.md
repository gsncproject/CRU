#cru #tcoym 

**Characters in this scene**: [[Jeremy Cade]], [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]], [[Jamie Taylor]]

At this point I hardly believe that someone sold me the wrong drugs. That I‘m on some LSD trip or something. The theater is huge, not as huge as any in which my plays have been performed… like Radio City Hall Theater in New York… damn, I want to make it on Broadway. 

Back to the fact that on my apparent LSD trip I‘m now sitting in this huge theater. As I look around I realize that I am alone. No other living and breathing thing is here with me. The stage is dimly lit as if there was a performance about to happen. If this is one of Korra‘s pranks I‘m offically sueing her, because how can she do that?

Then a kid walks on stage, blonde hair, curls, and he definitely couldn‘t be more than twelve years old. He stares right at me, I stare back. Neither of us say anything, because what do you say in this situation? Kid walks to the edge of the stage and simply sits down, still staring at me. Is he about to lecture me or what? His tiny legs start swinging slighly. And I do not know what to do with a kid that is staring at me. What do you do when a kid stares at you? Any advice?

„You‘re the writer?“ the boy asks, and he sounds almost sad. God, who hurt this sweet boy? 

I nod, „Yep, Jeremy. And you?“ This was the worst way to start a conversation. Help me out of my misery, dear God.

The boy seems to completely ignore my question, and starts fidgeting with his hoodie sleeves, „Do you ever get scared?“

Sure, I get scared. All the time. Who does get scared? Why does he ask that? I run a hand through my hair, „Uhm, scared of what, mate?“

„Death?“

Death? Bloody death? I already thought that kid is scary, now its even scarier. How can I leave? I need to leave before he comes at me to murder me. „Erm…“ I don‘t know what to tell him. I think he‘s going to murder me isn‘t he?

Almost scared the kid pushes himself up to his feet and glances towards the wings, „He‘s here…“ he whispers almost intelligable. This is getting more creepy every time that boy opens his mouth. But before I could open my mouth a clapping echoes through the empty theater hall. I glance behind me, no one. Around me, no one. My eyes find the stage again, and there was a man standing next to the kid. Wearing a fedora. I hate fedoras.

The man smiles, „Kid‘s got a habit of startin‘ before the real start,“ he steps closer to the boy, „Still sulkin‘? Can‘t help ya if you bite the dust.“ Is that guy Texan? At this point I feel like there is no way that this guy is an innocent man, right? Did he murder the boy?

„Who the hell are you?“ I manage. This has to be a bad LSD trip. 

He raises his finger slightly and shakes his head almost invisible, „You oughta be askin‘ who that boy is.“ His smile remains as he lays a hand on the boys shoulder. The kid looks terrified. 

„Uhm,“ I stand up, „Nah, I‘m very certain I asked the right question.“ And I‘m also certain that this man just came straight out of a 1950‘s movie about two very famous outlaws… Great, my mind is creating characters without me. 

The man chuckles, „Thinkin‘ this‘ a dream or somethin‘?“

„That, or I‘m dead,“ I smile back uneasy. He really is intimidating. I wouldn‘t wonder if he confesses that he has murder ten people. Or thirty. But I really hope he murdered none, because if he did I can be his next victim- Shut up, Jeremy!
