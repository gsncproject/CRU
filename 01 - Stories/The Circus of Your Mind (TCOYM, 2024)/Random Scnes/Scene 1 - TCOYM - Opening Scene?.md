The empty notebook page stares at me. I stare back, then I stare harder and yet no words appear. Not on page not in my head, which by the way is rude. Like incredibly rude. tThere are people out there who root for me and still I can’t get my shitty brain to make up another good story.

So what? That one great play of Remember Me is all I could write? Honestly, I might as well quit my job. Samuel will kill me if I don’t deliver in a week… and I need my life, just saying.

I pick up the pen once more and write „Sam made eggs. Again. For breakfast.“, crossed out immediately, then I write „Fuck me.“ and cross it out once again. Though, „Fuck Me“ isn’t the next award-winning show either. Honestly, very sad because it could pay for something better than whatever this apartment is supposed to be.

My phone buzzes. God knows I know ist Korra. You know, Korra Thornton, great journalist, my best friend since grade six, terrifying and professional destroyer of my concentration when I don’t own it. God, I love her. Not literally, but as, you know, a friend? Okay, maybe literally, but don’t tell her.

Back to the fact that she calls me, because I should answer. Last time I didn’t answer she almost broke down my flat door, cost me £197 to replace it, not that I have much money anyway, and she didn’t even offer to pay… which relatable because I didn’t pay her when I broke her favorite mug. That’s why the best decision is to answer her, because I don’t want to lose more money because of her. I put on speaker so that I can continue my staring contest with the empty page.

„Jer,“ she starts, „Got a minute?“

Of course I don’t have a minute, hell I don’t have a second. I’m currently trying and failing to write a play, but she doesn’t need to know, right? „Sure,“ I mutter, „Sup?“

Paper rustles on her end. So, she’s probably at work. Show-off, be happy you have a reliable job, K, „Not much,“ she responds after a moment, „See, Samuel called,“

„Korra—“

„No, Jeremy,“ she cuts me off, „He said he’ll drop you if you don’t get him another story by Tuesday. You’re gonna loose your job, you know that?“

No, I didn’t know— I do know, what the fuck do you think? I love her but she is very annoying and very stubborn at times, like right now! „It’s Thursday! I’ve been trying to come up with something, Korra,“ I try, „But my mind just keeps deciding to quit the cooperation.“

That at least was true, with the twist that my mind already quitted after releasing my last play. Which was, hold on… twenty-one months ago. Wow, almost two years. Record. Now I see why Sam hates me. I would hate me too.
