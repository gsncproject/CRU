**Characters in this scene**: [[Jeremy Cade]], [[Finn Kaelen]], [[Korra Thornton]] (mentioned)

Mornings always start with coffee. And if you‘re not a coffee person, you‘re missing out. Like so much.

The café down the street from my flat is always filled during the morning hours, which is when I plant myself at a table with coffee and my notebook.

Only today… I don‘t feel like writing. The last thing I wrote was how Jamie left after his argument with his sister Sam. Just like last night went…

„Finn and I are dating—“

Weird that I don‘t feel bad for telling Finn to go and fuck himself. But it is weird that I just stormed out of my best friends flat. I don‘t even know if I can ever face her again. I love her and she loves… him.

I remember in year five when we made that stupid promise that if we never find a partner we‘d just marry. As best friends. 

Aged well.

I keep re-reading that one paragraph in my notebook, „Never. Not today, or tomorrow or tomorrow-tomorrow.“ This is so stupid.

“This seat taken?“ a voice asks.

I look up it‘s Finn. Without even hesitating I grab my notebook and pack my stuff to leave.

Finn sits down across from me, „H-Hey, wait. I just want to talk.“

„Right,“ I mutter, „Rubbing in my face that you and K are dating, hm?“

He looks genuienly confused now, „No?“, he leans on the table, „I wanted to check on you after yesterday.“

I stop, look at them and try to find the lie. 

„Why?“

„Because…“ Finn sighs, „I‘m not your enemy. No, Korra keeps talking about you. A lot.“

„What?“

„Yeah,“ he smiles, „She always says she‘s proud of you. That you‘re… you know, like a big brother to her.“

Big brother…? That‘s all? I‘m just like a big brother for her? I don‘t know if I should feel flattered or bullied.

Finn continues, „It‘s often just… ‘Jeremy here, Jeremy that‘. And mate, it‘s obvious you love her too.“

I look away, „Mkay.“ Never thought it was that obvious.

„I just want you to know, that I won‘t get in between your and Korra‘s friendship,“ he says, „I have nothing against it.“

But I have a lot against you, F-I-N-N. 

I get up and take my bag, „So now that it‘s settled, au revior.“ 

I turn and leave.
