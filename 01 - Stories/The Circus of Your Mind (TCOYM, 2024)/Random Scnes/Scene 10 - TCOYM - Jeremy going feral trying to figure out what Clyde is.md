**Characters in this scene**: [[Jeremy Cade]], [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]] (mentioned)

Another thing no one told me when I became a playwright was that I would have to deal with unhumanly annoying non existant dead outlaws. Guess the job discription just… changed? Improved? Or fucked my life? Maybe all three.

In short: Do not become a playwright or you‘ll lose your sanity. Which I did years ago. I should‘ve listened to Uncle Gary and become a mechanic or a librarian. My life would be so much easier and I‘d have stable income. I believe.

It is almost ten at night by now and I realise that whatever Clyde is, has to be other earthly. How else do I see him my dreams? Why does my fridge hum so loud? I‘m going bloody insane. Still, both questions have no answer. Or as Clyde would put it „Yeah“.

A healthy person would go to bed by now, but lucky me I‘m not a healthy person, I lost about ninty-seven percent of my sanity in the last twenty-four hours. And I miss my sanity, can someone give me theirs? I need it more than money. Actually I take money too, because money makes happy, did you know that? 

That being said, I will not sleep a single second tonight. And I have an idea how to kill time. Somewhere in my drawer there has to be an empty notebook— HA! Found it! I find my desk chair or well… it finds me, pick up a pen and start writing.

Title: “What are you?“ Underlined of course. This has to seem professional.

„Ghost?“ crossed out.

„Demon?“ crossed out, rewritten. I don‘t know if he is a demon, but if he is then its a very nice one. I heard different stuff from TV shows and movies, you know? 

„Tupla?“ crossed out, again? What the fuck is a Tulpa? 

The more I think about it I have absolutely no bloody idea what Clyde could be. As of now he‘s just… a dumb dream hallucination that won‘t shut up and looks stupid with that fedora. And of course he scares the little boy.

I need research. And I don‘t know where my laptop is. Great. Awesome. My phone‘s doing great. I open Google and type: „What kind of being is a man with a fedora that you only see in your dreams?“. The answer: „Go seek a psychologist.“ 

Might as well.
