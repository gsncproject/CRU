# The Jamie Taylor Case

## Premise
One week after the events of [[Clyde Rules - Overview|Clyde Rules]], [[Sam Taylor]], Jamie's older sister, teams up with her best friend [[Winston Desjardins]] and new student [[Wren Carter]] to investigate the death of Jamie Taylor. Jamie's death is publicly known, but nobody publicly knows who killed him or the full circumstances. [[Detective Richard Thornton]] killed Jamie after Jamie attempted to kill him, then hid the evidence of his involvement. Sam, Winn, and Wren try to uncover what really happened.

## Plot Summary
Sam, Winn, and Wren investigate Jamie's death because Detective Richard Thornton has concealed evidence showing that he killed Jamie. Their investigation attempts to reconstruct what happened during the confrontation between Richard and Jamie and uncover the truth that was hidden from the public.
