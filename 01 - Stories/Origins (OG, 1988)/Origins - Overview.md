## Plot Summary
[[Director Aiden Janson]] lives with his uncle [[Peter Janson]] and tries to find either a job or a college that accepts him. Until one day when he found a job advertisement that actually sounded like something for him, but he didn't know it was for the governmental agency [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]]. 

He applies and gets an interview where he meets [[Director Howard Beckham]]. Beckham tells him that [[Director Aiden Janson]] is that kind of guy they were looking for.

The first few weeks at [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]] are boring before [[Director Aiden Janson]] gets his first actual mission: to extract some object from a black-site of their enemies. The mission starts good and easy at first until it doesn't. The object breaks during a confrontation with [[Maurice Curtis]] and whatever was trapped inside was now free. After eventually neutralizing the threat and [[Director Aiden Janson]] being traumatized to his core, him and his team move on and try to find whatever was trapped inside that object to lock it back up.

The team splits and secures the parameters of the black-site and [[Director Aiden Janson]] walks through the facility by himself determined to find that thing and show that he is not some scrawny kid. Then he comes across a guy who from behind looked like he stepped out of the [[1930]]'s... eventually its [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]], or the thing disguising as him. There is a small confrontation before [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]] disappears and [[Director Aiden Janson]] thinks he's killed it.

Later back at the [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]] [[Director Aiden Janson]] and his team report back to [[Director Howard Beckham]], but something seems off... and [[Director Aiden Janson]] is the only one who notices that there is something wrong or at least he thinks there is something wrong. 

It all ends in another confrontation where [[Director Howard Beckham]] or whatever it is trying to kill [[Director Aiden Janson]], and it turns out that it is the same thing that disguised itself as [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]]... so later when that thing again disappears and [[Director Aiden Janson]] comes across the actual [[Director Howard Beckham]] he kills him thinking its the thing...
