# 1988 Clyde Incident

## Overview
The 1988 Clyde Incident was Aiden Janson's first mission as an ARA recruit and the event that released Clyde from his containment.

## Events
- ARA sent recruit Aiden Janson to retrieve an object stored at a MAC blacksite.
- The object was a clay box containing Clyde.
- Director John Corson had originally imprisoned Clyde in the box because Clyde was considered a threat to humanity.
- Janson was attacked during the mission and the clay box broke.
- Clyde was released.
- Clyde subsequently impersonated Director Howard Beckham in an attempt to harm Janson.
- After returning to Washington, Janson encountered Beckham and believed he was Clyde.
- Janson killed Beckham.
- Janson was detained and tried for Beckham's death.
- The circumstances included psychological torture, and Janson was ultimately found innocent.
- Following the incident, Janson became the Director of ARA.

## Historical Significance
The incident became one of the defining events in ARA's history and the beginning of Janson's long-term involvement with Clyde. ARA subsequently continued trying to locate Clyde.

## Public Knowledge
The incident was covered up and is not publicly known as it actually happened.
