# 1947 — [[Marble Arch Council|MAC]] Founded

[[Marble Arch Council|MAC]] was founded in 1947 by Maurice Curtis and his best friend Friedrich Ziegel.

## Original Purpose
[[Marble Arch Council|MAC]] was created to expose government secrets from World War II and the post-war period.

## Early Development
[[Marble Arch Council|MAC]] continued exposing secrets and eventually discovered that supernatural phenomena exist.

[[Marble Arch Council|MAC]] later came into conflict with [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]]. Around 1975, [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]] and [[Marble Arch Council|MAC]] became aware of each other and became enemies. [[Marble Arch Council|MAC]] has repeatedly infiltrated [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]] and maintains undercover agents within it.
