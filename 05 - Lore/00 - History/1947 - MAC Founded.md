# 1947 — MAC Founded

MAC was founded in 1947 by Maurice Curtis and his best friend Friedrich Ziegel.

## Original Purpose
MAC was created to expose government secrets from World War II and the post-war period.

## Early Development
MAC continued exposing secrets and eventually discovered that supernatural phenomena exist.

MAC later came into conflict with ARA. Around 1975, ARA and MAC became aware of each other and became enemies. MAC has repeatedly infiltrated ARA and maintains undercover agents within it.
