# 00 - History Hub

The historical backbone of the CRU. This folder records real-world historical events as they occurred in the CRU, supernatural incidents hidden within history, the development of ARA and MAC, and major CRU-original historical events.

## Historical Categories
- [[01 - Ancient & Early History]] — Early historical events and the origins of supernatural activity.
- [[02 - Pre-ARA History]] — Supernatural and governmental history before ARA's founding in 1962.
- [[03 - Post-WWII History]] — The aftermath of World War II and the rise of MAC.
- [[04 - ARA History]] — The founding and development of the Advanced Research Agency.
- [[05 - MAC History]] — The history and activities of MAC.
- [[06 - Supernatural History]] — Known supernatural events throughout human history.
- [[07 - Clyde History]] — Historical events involving Clyde and his manifestations.
- [[08 - 1988 Clyde Incident]] — Janson's first mission and Clyde's release from containment.
- [[09 - Modern History]] — Major modern historical events and their CRU implications.

## Core Historical Timeline
- **c. 1817 — Bell Witch:** Earliest known supernatural event in CRU history.
- **1947 — MAC founded:** Maurice Curtis and his best friend Friedrich Ziegel establish MAC to expose government secrets from WWII and its aftermath.
- **1947 — Roswell Incident:** The public's first major exposure to the possibility of extraterrestrial life.
- **1959 — Dyatlov Pass Incident:** Potential historical supernatural case to investigate within CRU canon.
- **1962 — ARA founded:** The U.S. Government establishes ARA. Director John Corson becomes its first director.
- **1964 — Souls confirmed:** ARA research establishes that souls exist.
- **~1975 — ARA/MAC conflict:** ARA and MAC become aware of each other and become enemies. MAC begins infiltrating ARA, including through undercover agents.
- **1974–1976 — Amityville:** Potential supernatural case to integrate into CRU history.
- **1980 — Rendlesham Forest Incident:** Potential ARA/UK Branch historical case.
- **1986 — Chernobyl:** Potential historical supernatural case occurring shortly before the Clyde Incident.
- **1988 — Clyde Incident:** ARA recruit Aiden Janson retrieves a clay box from a MAC blacksite; the box breaks and Clyde is released.
- **2012 — Elena Rostova becomes Director-General:** Elena takes over the highest position in ARA.
- **2001 — 9/11:** Major historical event with a CRU-side history still to be established.

## Historical Event Template
For historical supernatural or mysterious events, record:
- Event
- Approx. year
- Location
- What happened
- Supernatural thing/entity involved
- What ARA/MAC did about it
- Public explanation
- Survivors / people who know the truth

Not every historical event needs to contain supernatural activity. Some events are included simply because they are important to the CRU's version of real-world history.
