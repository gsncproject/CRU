# 1962 — ARA Founded

The Advanced Research Agency (ARA) was founded by the U.S. Government in 1962. Its first director was Director John Corson.

## Original Purpose
ARA was originally created to research supernatural phenomena.

## Early Supernatural Research
By 1964, ARA research had established that souls exist. ARA later became involved in identifying and neutralizing supernatural beings when necessary, while attempting to locate Clyde.

## Relationship with MAC
ARA and MAC became aware of each other around 1975 and became enemies. MAC has infiltrated ARA multiple times, including through undercover agents. ARA has also infiltrated MAC, though less frequently.
