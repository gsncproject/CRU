# CRU Universe Rules

## Multiverse
- The Clyde Rules Universe is a **multiverse**.
- The stories exist within a connected wider continuity rather than as isolated continuities.
- Characters cross between stories frequently.
- Examples established by canon include [[Jeremy Cade]] appearing in [[Weight of Tomorrow - Overview|Weight of Tomorrow]] after [[The Circus of Your Mind - Overview|The Circus of Your Mind]], and [[Wren Carter]] crossing from [[I Couldn‘t Fix It - Overview|I Couldn't Fix It]] into [[The Jamie Taylor Case - Overview|The Jamie Taylor Case]].
- Characters in the same story are assumed to have met unless explicitly established otherwise.

## Supernatural Phenomena
- Supernatural entities exist.
- Ghosts can exist, but not necessarily according to conventional ideas of ghosts.
- [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]] is currently best described as a supernatural entity. His exact nature remains unknown; current possibilities are closest to a tulpa, egregore, or djinn-like entity.
- Clyde can manifest through hallucinations and/or dreams.
- Clyde can create experiences inside another person's mind.
- Clyde's dream-theater experienced by [[Jeremy Cade]] is created by Clyde inside Jeremy's head.
- Whether [[Jamie Taylor]] appears as an actual ghost after his death, or whether his appearances in [[The Circus of Your Mind - Overview|The Circus of Your Mind]] are a manifestation created by Clyde, is currently unresolved.

## Visions and the Future
- Characters can experience visions of events that have not yet happened.
- [[Luis de la Fluente]] is an established example: he sees future events, including the future death of his sister [[Ally de la Fluente]].
- The future can potentially be affected by attempts to change it; the exact rules are not yet fully established.

## Alien Life
- Aliens exist in CRU.
- The general public has some awareness that aliens exist, but does not know the full truth.
- The [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]] researches and deals with extraterrestrial phenomena.

## The ARA
- The [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]] is a secret governmental organization.
- The general public does not know that the ARA exists.
- The ARA has international branches.
- The [[Washington Branch]] is above the NYC Branch.
- [[Director-General Elena Rostova]] directs the Washington Branch and is above [[Director Aiden Janson]].
- [[Director Aiden Janson]] directs the NYC Branch.

## Clyde's Origin
- The entity now known as [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]] originates from the events surrounding [[Origins - Overview|Origins]].
- [[Director Aiden Janson]] is one of the reasons why Clyde now exists.
- The exact mechanism by which Clyde came into existence remains unknown.

## Death and Secrecy
- A person's death can be publicly known while the circumstances surrounding it remain secret or disputed.
- [[Jamie Taylor]]'s death is publicly known, but the fact that [[Detective Richard Thornton]] killed him is not publicly known at the beginning of [[The Jamie Taylor Case - Overview|The Jamie Taylor Case]].
- Richard hid evidence of his involvement.

## Storytelling / Reality
- [[Jeremy Cade]] unknowingly writes about real events and people in the USA while living in the UK.
- His writing is based on events that actually happen rather than being purely fictional.
- While writing Jamie's story, Jeremy unknowingly writes about a real person.
- [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]] wants Jeremy to write Jamie's story and appears to him through dreams.

## Unknown / Still Developing
- The exact nature of Clyde.
- The exact rules governing ghosts and post-death manifestations.
- The full mechanics of future visions and changing the future.
- The complete structure and capabilities of the multiverse.
- The full purpose and history of the Spain Branch.
