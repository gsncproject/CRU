# CRU Timeline

> A chronological overview of the known events of the Clyde Rules Universe. Dates that have not been established are marked as **TBD** rather than guessed.

## 1988 — Origins

### Washington, D.C. — Aiden Janson joins the ARA
- [[Director Aiden Janson]] is living with his uncle, [[Peter Janson]], and looking for either a job or a college.
- Aiden applies to the [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]] without initially realizing it is a governmental agency.
- He is interviewed by [[Director Howard Beckham]] and hired.
- After several uneventful weeks, Aiden receives his first major ARA mission: extracting an object from an enemy black site.

### The Black-Site Incident
- Aiden's team enters the black site to retrieve the object.
- During a confrontation with [[Maurice Curtis]], the object breaks.
- Whatever was trapped inside the object is released.
- Aiden and his team attempt to locate and contain the escaped entity.
- Aiden encounters a figure resembling [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]], apparently a man from the 1930s. The figure is actually the escaped entity disguising itself as Clyde.
- Aiden believes he has neutralized the entity after the confrontation.

### The Beckham Incident
- Back at the ARA, Aiden notices that [[Director Howard Beckham]] is behaving strangely.
- The escaped entity later reveals itself again while disguised as Beckham and attempts to kill Aiden.
- Aiden eventually encounters the real Howard Beckham.
- Believing Beckham is still the entity, Aiden kills him.
- **Result:** Aiden's first major ARA mission becomes the defining traumatic event of his early career and the foundation of his future as ARA Director.

**Story:** [[Origins - Overview|Origins]]

## 2024

### [[Clyde Rules - Overview|Clyde Rules]]
- **Year:** 2024
- **Location:** [[Northbridge, NY (CRU)]]
- Twelve-year-old [[Jamie Taylor]] begins hallucinating/encountering [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]].
- Jamie increasingly trusts Clyde over authority figures.
- Jamie's behavior becomes increasingly reckless as his relationship with Clyde develops.
- The events ultimately lead to Jamie's death.
- [[Detective Richard Thornton]] and the [[Northbridge Police Dept.]] are involved in the surrounding events.

### [[Don‘t Forget - Overview|Don't Forget]]
- **Year:** 2024
- **Location:** [[New York City, NY (CRU)]]
- [[Curtis Jensen]] is pulled into serious trouble by his friend [[Keith Duz]].
- The two become involved in a murder and the consequences that follow.
- Exact date and placement within 2024 are **TBD**.

### [[Remember Me - Overview|Remember Me]]
- **Year:** 2024
- **Location:** [[San Francisco, CA (CRU)]]
- [[Cade Clay Buchanan]], [[Dylan Thornton]], and [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]] are connected to the events.
- Exact chronology and dates are **TBD**.

### [[The Circus of Your Mind - Overview|The Circus of Your Mind]]
- **Year:** 2024
- **Location:** [[London, UK (CRU)]]
- [[Jeremy Cade]], a successful young playwright/author, begins searching for a completely new story.
- [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]] begins appearing to Jeremy, including through strange dream/theater experiences.
- [[Jamie Taylor]] appears in one of Jeremy's dream-theater encounters.
- Jeremy becomes increasingly obsessed with determining what Clyde actually is.
- Jeremy eventually suspects that Clyde is something otherworldly rather than simply a hallucination.
- [[Finn Kaelen]] is revealed to be an American government agent, creating a direct connection between Jeremy's London story and the wider governmental side of CRU.
- [[Loenna Thornton]] and [[Korra Thornton]] are involved in Jeremy's life and the unfolding events.
- Exact date and placement within 2024 are **TBD**.

**Order between the 2024 stories:** TBD.

## 2025

### Late June – Early July 2025 — [[A Techies Guide to Trouble - Overview|A Techie's Guide to Trouble]]
- **Location:** [[New York City, NY (CRU)]]
- [[Kieran Jamie Shaw]], [[Corey Gatsby]], [[Kyra Aldun]], and [[John Curtis]] become caught up in events far beyond Kieran's expectations.

### One week later — [[Weight of Tomorrow - Overview|Weight of Tomorrow]]
- **Location:** [[New York City, NY (CRU)]]
- [[Jeremy Cade]] is being held in "containment" by the [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]].

- [[Finn Kaelen]], [[Director Aiden Janson]], [[Seymour Collins]], and [[Lieutenant Ariana Voss]] are also involved.
- The story takes place exactly **one week after** [[A Techies Guide to Trouble - Overview|A Techie's Guide to Trouble]].
