# [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]] Structure Ideas

## General
- [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]] is an international organization with multiple Regional Headquarters.
- Washington D.C. serves as the Global Headquarters.
- Every Regional HQ has its own Director, departments, labs, and black sites.
- Regional HQs cooperate but have their own specialties and culture.

## Possible Regional HQs
- Washington D.C. – Global Administration / Leadership
- New York City – Research & Technology
- Los Angeles – Field Operations & Recruitment
- San Francisco – Intelligence & Cybersecurity
- London – European Division / Oneiric (Dream) Research

## Black Sites
- Every Regional HQ operates several classified black sites.
- Black sites specialize in containment, research, interrogation, artifact storage, etc.
- Official names and agent nicknames may differ.

## Internal Culture
- Regional HQs have friendly rivalries.
- Different branches develop different methods and traditions.
- Agents are occasionally transferred between branches.

## Story Potential
- Characters can be reassigned between HQs.
- Different stories can naturally take place at different branches.
- Allows future expansion without changing the overall structure.
