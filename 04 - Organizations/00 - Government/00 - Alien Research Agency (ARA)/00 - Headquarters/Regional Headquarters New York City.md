# Regional Headquarters New York City
## Basic Information
- Official Name: Alien Research Agency Regional Headquarters New York City
- Common Name: NYC HQ
- Type: Regional Headquarters
- Location: New York City, NY
- Established: 1984
- Director: [[Director Aiden Janson]]
- Jurisdiction: Northeastern United States

## Purpose
Coordinates ARA operations across the northeastern United States. The headquarters specializes in containment, intelligence gathering, technological research and rapid-response missions.
