# Global Headquarters Washington D.C
## Basic Information
- Official Name: Alien Research Agency Global Headquarters
- Common Name: Global HQ
- Type: Global Headquarters
- Location: Washington D.C., USA
- Established: 1945 
- Director: [[Director-General Elena Rostova]]
- Jurisdiction: Worldwide
- Status: Active
## Purpose
The Global Headquarters serves as the administrative and strategic center of the Alien Research Agency. It oversees all regional headquarters, coordinates international operations, approves high-level missions, and maintains the organization's central archives.
