## Premise
After experiencing a strange vision in which he cannot see his own hands, Luis de la Fluente begins experiencing increasingly disturbing glimpses of events that have not yet happened. As the visions begin pointing toward the death of his sister Ally, Luis becomes obsessed with understanding what they mean — and whether the future can be changed.

## Plot Summary
Luis begins experiencing visions of possible future events, eventually discovering that they appear to revolve around Ally's death. His attempts to prevent the events he sees only bring him closer to them, eventually culminating in Ally's murder.

Following Ally's death, the circumstances surrounding Luis's visions attract the attention of organizations interested in unexplained phenomena and information.
