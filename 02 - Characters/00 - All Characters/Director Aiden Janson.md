# Director Aiden Janson
## Basic Information
- Full Name: Aiden Janson
- Aliases: Janson
- Birthday: November 24th, 1967 — Brooklyn, NY
- Residence: [[New York City, NY (CRU)]]
- Occupation: Director of the New York City Branch of the [[Alien Research Agency]]
- Status: Alive
## Personality
Janson has a reputation for gaining his status in ARA through morally grey actions. He is strict and intimidating, but morally grey rather than simply evil. He can be protective and is especially protective of his daughter, Sage Carter.
## Appearance
Not established.
## Biography
Aiden Janson is the Director of the NYC Branch of ARA. [[Director-General Elena Rostova]] is above him in the hierarchy. Elena knows his history and considers him the right person to direct the NYC facility.

Janson was raised by his uncle [[Peter Janson]] after his parents died. He knows about [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]] and has personally encountered the entity. In 1988, Clyde was trapped in a clay box-like object that later broke and caused havoc. Clyde had previously impersonated [[Director Howard Beckham]], and the events ultimately caused Janson to murder Beckham.

Janson is the biological father of [[Sage Carter]]. Sage knows he is her father, and Janson knows she is his daughter. He protects Sage and later joins the Lab Four group in [[Weight of Tomorrow - Overview|Weight of Tomorrow]].
