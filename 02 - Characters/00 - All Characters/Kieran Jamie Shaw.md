# Jamie Shaw 
## Basic Information 
- Full Name: Kieran Jamie Shaw 
- Aliases: *Jamie*, Wynn, J, Agent Shaw 
- Birthday: October 21st 2004 
- Residence: [[New York City, NY (CRU)]]
- Occupation: Resident Tech at the [[Regional Headquarters New York City]] 
- Status: Alive 
## Personality 
Jamie appears as the usual nervous nerd but that the same time he has a very hard time understanding simple things, and then ends up in much more difficult situations. He is friendly, empathic, emotional.
## Appearance 
Jamie has short brown curls, green eyes and loves to wear hoodies with jeans and his favorite red Converse. His height is at about 174cm.
## Biography
### Before [[A Techies Guide to Trouble - Overview|ATGTT]]:
When Jamie was just two years old his parents died and his sister, [[Hayden Shaw]], had to raise him from then on. She made sure that Jamie had a normal childhood while Jamie was most reserved for himself and interessted in tech.

By the time he turned sixteen years old he already knew most of the tech stuff that was taught at universities. Then one year later just before his eighteenth birthday he went to college where he met his from-then-on best friend [[Oscar Zander]]. And soon after they moved into a small four-room apartment near the college campus.

And then shortly after his twentith birthday, due to a mistake he made while hacking governmental information, the [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]] found out about him and instead of arresting him [[Director Aiden Janson]] offered him a job as resident tech and tech support for the field agents. 

Arriving at [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]] he almost immediately was introduced to his main team ([[Kyra Aldun]] & [[Corey Gatsby]]) he would be working with for the time being, and it was the very same team he was the tech guy for. The three became good friends very quickly and Jamie and Corey often argued about tech problems while Kyra complained. Their „central base“ became [[Lab Four]], just like it became their second home.
### During [[A Techies Guide to Trouble - Overview|ATGTT]]:
At the beginning Jamie appears as the tech nerd asking too many wrong questions and seemingly not really having an idea as to how a governmental agency works, despite already working there for seven months. And Jamie himself knows that [[Director Aiden Janson]] is fed up with his behavior because he doesn‘t even try to make his annoyance not obvious. 

Dispite that Jamie‘s dynamic with his team members ([[Kyra Aldun]] & [[Corey Gatsby]]) seems naturally as if they had known each other forever. They bicker and argue like little kids and Corey keeps reminding him that he is not a legal adult yet. 

Then eventually Janson gives them the task to figure out who this new vigilate in NYC was, because they were seriously interfering with [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]]‘s work. While doing research on this, Jamie discovers more than weird stuff and before he knows it Janson basically fires him.

After being fired Jamie decides to find out who that vigilante is by his own until he eventually finds an investigation partner: [[Sage Carter]]. Sage, who herself is/was an [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]] agent shows Jamie how real field work works and encourages him to be more reckless.

Jamie takes that advice seriously and becomes the most reckless person you can imagine. So much that his own sister becomes concerned for her little brother. And Sage and Jamie together go after [[Marble Arch Council]]s two „leaders“ [[Winston Zander]] & [[John Curtis]].
