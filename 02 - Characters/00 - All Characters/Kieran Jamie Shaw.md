# Jamie Shaw
## Basic Information
- Full Name: Kieran Jamie Shaw
- Aliases: Jamie, Wynn, J, Agent Shaw
- Birthday: October 21st 2004
- Birthplace: Manhattan, NY
- Age: 19 (2024); 20 (2025)
- Residence: [[New York City, NY (CRU)]]
- Occupation: Resident Tech at the [[Regional Headquarters New York City]]
- Status: Alive
## Personality
Jamie is a nervous, friendly, empathic, and emotional tech prodigy who frequently struggles with seemingly simple things while being capable of highly advanced technical work. He asks too many questions, often misunderstands situations, and has a tendency to turn difficult situations into much bigger ones. Despite his nervousness, he can become extremely reckless when he commits to something.

He has a close, chaotic friendship with [[Kyra Aldun]] and [[Corey Gatsby]]. He is also deeply attached to his sister [[Hayden Shaw]] and has a strong friendship with [[Oscar Zander]]. Jamie is an absolute theater kid despite his strong focus on technology.
## Appearance
Jamie has short brown curls, green eyes, and is about 174 cm tall. He likes hoodies and jeans and regularly wears his favorite red Converse.
## Biography
When Jamie was two years old, his parents died and his older sister, [[Hayden Shaw]], raised him. She tried to give him a normal childhood while he became increasingly interested in technology. Jamie was very reserved and focused on tech from a young age.

By sixteen, he already knew most of the technical material taught at universities. At seventeen he went to college, where he met his best friend [[Oscar Zander]]. The two eventually moved into a small four-room apartment near campus.

Shortly after turning twenty, Jamie made a mistake while hacking governmental information. The [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]] discovered him. Instead of arresting him, [[Director Aiden Janson]] offered him a position as resident tech and technical support for field agents.

At the ARA, Jamie was introduced to his main team, [[Kyra Aldun]] and [[Corey Gatsby]]. The three quickly became close friends. Their central base became [[Lab Four]], which also became a second home for the group.

### During [[A Techies Guide to Trouble - Overview|A Techies Guide to Trouble]]
Jamie has already been working for the agency for seven months when the story begins, but still seems unsure about how a governmental agency works. Janson is visibly annoyed by his behavior. Jamie's dynamic with Kyra and Corey remains naturally close and argumentative.

Janson assigns the team to identify a vigilante operating in New York City who is interfering with ARA work. During his research Jamie discovers increasingly strange information and is eventually fired by Janson.

After being fired, Jamie continues investigating on his own and finds an investigation partner in [[Sage Carter]], an ARA agent or former agent. Sage teaches him about real field work and encourages him to be more reckless. Jamie takes that advice to an extreme and becomes increasingly reckless, worrying Hayden.

Jamie and Sage eventually pursue the two leaders of the [[Marble Arch Council]], [[Winston Zander]] and [[John Curtis]].
