# Director Aiden Janson
- Full Name: Aiden Janson
- Birthday: November 24, 1967 — Brooklyn, NY
- Role: Director of the ARA NYC Branch
- Organization: ARA
- Status: Alive
- 1988: As a recruit, his first mission was retrieving a clay box from a MAC blacksite. The box broke after he was attacked, freeing Clyde. Janson later mistook Clyde-impersonating Howard Beckham for Clyde and killed Beckham. He was detained and tried, but found innocent after psychological torture was considered; he subsequently became ARA Director.
- Janson knows Clyde exists and knows the 1988 incident.
- Elena Rostova is above him as ARA Director-General. Elena does not exactly like him, but considers him the right person to direct NYC.
- He has a morally grey reputation and gained status through morally grey actions.
- Sage Carter is his secret daughter; both know their relationship.
- Peter Janson is his uncle and raised him after his parents died.
- Janson is involved with Lab Four in WOT.
