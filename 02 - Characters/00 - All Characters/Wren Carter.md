## Biography
Wren Carter becomes the new student at Sam Taylor's school after the events of [[I Couldn‘t Fix It - Overview|I Couldn't Fix It]]. At lunch, [[Sam Taylor]] and [[Winston Desjardins]] are talking and sitting together when Wren simply joins them.
Wren becomes involved in investigating [[Jamie Taylor]]'s death because they like mysteries. Wren does not believe Sam's claims about Jamie communicating with her, does not see Jamie, and does not encounter [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]].
Wren and [[Sage Carter]] are cousins.
