# Kade Bogart
## Basic Information
- Full Name: Kade Whittaker
- Alias: Kade Bogart
- Birthday: March 19th, 2008
- Birthplace: Dayton, Ohio, USA
- Age: 15 in [[Autumn - Overview|Autumn]] (2023)
- Residence: Reseda, Los Angeles, CA, USA
- Occupation: Student
- Status: Alive
- Role: Protagonist; narrator

## Appearance
- Brown hair
- Brown-green eyes

## Personality
Kade is quiet, sarcastic, chaotic, and sometimes an asshole. He tries to be nice, but if he has to be an asshole, he will be one. He is very bad at lying. Despite claiming that he does not trust people easily, he actually tends to trust people quickly.

He is deeply afraid of dying and of being found by his parents or the police. He is also terrified of the supernatural.

## Biography
Kade Whittaker ran away from his home in Ohio at the age of thirteen. He disliked his life as a wealthy child and the expectations surrounding it, and eventually left home. Since then, he has been living illegally in a motel in Reseda while attending Reseda High School.

In [[Autumn - Overview|Autumn]], Kade becomes involved with Agent Harry Beaumont, who finds him and recruits him into a situation connected to ARA. Kade is eventually betrayed by Beaumont and has to find a way out. He survives, although the experience leaves him with additional trauma.

Kade's real name is Kade Whittaker; "Kade Bogart" is an alias he uses.
