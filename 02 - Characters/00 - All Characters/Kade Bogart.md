# Kade Bogart
## Basic Information 
- Full Name: Kade Whittaker
- Aliases: Kade Bogart
- Birthday: March 19th, 2008
- Residence: [[Reseda, CA (CRU)]]
- Occupation: Student
- Status: Alive
## Personality 
Kade is quiet, sarcastic, chaotic, and sometimes an asshole. He tries to be nice, but if he has to be an asshole, he'll be one. He is very bad at lying. Despite claiming that he doesn't trust people easily, he actually tends to trust people quickly.

He is deeply afraid of dying, seeing his parents, the police, and the supernatural.
## Appearance 
- Brown hair
- Brown-green eyes
## Biography
Kade Whittaker ran away from home in Ohio at the age of thirteen. He hated the life of being a wealthy child and the expectations surrounding it. Since then, he has been living illegally in a motel in Reseda while attending Reseda High School.

In [[Autumn - Overview|Autumn]], Kade becomes involved with Agent Harry Beaumont, who finds and recruits him. He is eventually betrayed by Beaumont and manages to get out. The experience leaves him with additional trauma.

Kade's real name is Kade Whittaker; "Kade Bogart" is an alias he uses.
