# Hayden Shaw

## Personality
Hayden is established as a caring older sister who took responsibility for raising [[Kieran Jamie Shaw]] after their parents died. She tried to give Jamie a normal childhood while supporting his interest in technology. She remains protective of him, particularly when his behavior becomes increasingly reckless.

## Biography
When [[Kieran Jamie Shaw]] was two years old, their parents died and Hayden had to raise him. She made sure he had as normal a childhood as possible while he became increasingly interested in technology.

Hayden is a Broadway actress. She took Jamie along to her performances when he was younger; until the age of five, Jamie believed that Hayden was his mother.

By the time of [[A Techies Guide to Trouble - Overview|A Techies Guide to Trouble]], Hayden is an adult living in New York City and remains closely concerned with Jamie's wellbeing. Jamie's increasingly reckless investigation into the [[Marble Arch Council]] worries her because he is her little brother.
