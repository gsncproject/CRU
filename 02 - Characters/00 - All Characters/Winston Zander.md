## Biography
Winston Zander is the father of [[Oscar Zander]] and one of the two directors of MAC alongside [[John Curtis]]. He is deeply involved with MAC and both believes in its purpose and has personal reasons for his actions.
Winston is an antagonist in the CRU. His position makes him one of the people directing MAC and places him directly against ARA and the protagonists of [[A Techies Guide to Trouble - Overview|A Techies Guide to Trouble]].
