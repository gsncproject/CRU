## Biography
John Corson was the first Director of ARA. The agency was founded in 1962 by the U.S. Government and was originally intended to research supernatural phenomena.

Corson was responsible for imprisoning [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]] in a clay box because Clyde was considered a threat to humanity. The object was eventually stored at a MAC blacksite until [[Director Aiden Janson]]'s first mission in 1988, when Janson was ordered to retrieve it. The mission went wrong and the object broke, releasing Clyde.
