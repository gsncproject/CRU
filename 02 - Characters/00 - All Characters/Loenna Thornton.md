## Personality
Loenna is a theater kid who loves theater and loves to bicker with [[Jeremy Cade]]. She sees Jeremy as something like an older brother. She genuinely cares about the quality of his plays and criticizes him partly to prepare him for what his producer [[Samuel Barnes]] might say.
## Appearance

## Biography
Loenna Thornton is seventeen and lives with her older sister [[Korra Thornton]]. Their father is in prison and their mother is an alcoholic. Loenna is heavily involved in theater and becomes a significant personal connection for Jeremy in [[The Circus of Your Mind - Overview|The Circus of Your Mind]].
Loenna knows the story Jeremy is writing because he shares it with her, but she does not know that his play describes real events happening in the United States. She does not know about ARA.
