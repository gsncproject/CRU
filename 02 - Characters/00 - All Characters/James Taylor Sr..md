James Taylor Sr. was a New York City police officer, the husband of [[Mary Taylor]], and the father of [[Sam Taylor]] and [[Jamie Taylor]]. He died in action when Jamie was two years old.

Sam remembers him as an exceptionally good father who made sure his children had a father who genuinely cared about them.
