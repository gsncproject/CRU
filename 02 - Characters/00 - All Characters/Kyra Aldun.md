## Personality
Kyra is an experienced field agent and part of the close-knit team surrounding [[Kieran Jamie Shaw]] and [[Corey Gatsby]]. She frequently has to put up with the pair's bickering and chaos. She is also established as a trusted ally of Jamie and Corey.

## Biography
Kyra Diana Aldun works as a field agent for the [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]] at the [[Regional Headquarters New York City]]. She works closely with [[Kieran Jamie Shaw]] and [[Corey Gatsby]], and the three become good friends.

During [[A Techies Guide to Trouble - Overview|A Techies Guide to Trouble]], Kyra is part of the team assigned to investigate the vigilante interfering with ARA operations in New York City. Jamie and Corey frequently argue about technical problems while Kyra complains about their bickering.

Kyra's established CRU connections extend beyond ATGTT through [[I Couldn‘t Fix It - Overview|I Couldn‘t Fix It]], [[Weight of Tomorrow - Overview|Weight of Tomorrow]], and [[The Jamie Taylor Case - Overview|The Jamie Taylor Case]].
