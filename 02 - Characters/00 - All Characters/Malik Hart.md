## Biography
Malik Hart is [[Clara Hart]]'s older brother and owns the bar **Old Golden Inn** in the Broadway District. Officially he has no ARA position, but he is unofficially connected to ARA and knows some information about its activities and the supernatural side of the CRU.
In [[ATGTT - Overview|ATGTT]], Malik is part of the group trying to catch Oscar. The Old Golden Inn serves as a recurring meeting place for [[Kieran Jamie Shaw]], [[Kyra Aldun]], [[Corey Gatsby]], [[Hayden Shaw]], and Malik.
