# Oliver Orlando
## Basic Information
- Full Name: Oliver Orlando
- Aliases: Molotov, Olli (by his father Robert)
- Birthday:
- Residence:
- Occupation: Informant
- Status: Alive
## Personality
Molotov is sarcastic, cheeky, and usually wears a smirk. He is observant and knows more than he lets on. He sometimes mocks what his clients ask of him, and when he is mocking a client he may perform a theatrical bow before leaving. Being around him can make people feel as though they are being watched. When he dislikes or distrusts someone, he often clicks his tongue; he also uses this as a signal to his father that he does not trust someone present in a meeting.

He does not particularly care where his money comes from as long as he gets paid. Despite his involvement in the criminal world, police generally consider him harmless and have stopped arresting him.

## Biography
Not much is known about Molotov. He is often seen around abandoned buildings and crime scenes, usually watching and sneaking around. He does informant work for money and has a reputation for knowing more than he tells.

Molotov grew up around mafia life because his father, [[Robert Orlando]], has been involved in it since before Molotov was born. He was introduced to explosives at a young age and immediately loved them; the first explosive he became fascinated with was a Molotov cocktail, which inspired his nickname.

Molotov is Robert's son, although [[Lee Mueller]] initially does not know this. Robert brings Molotov along when he and Lee have to meet because of the case, before introducing Lee as his lawyer. Molotov mocks Lee during these meetings. He initially does not like Lee and clicks his tongue around him. Once Molotov learns that Lee knows about Robert's involvement in the mafia, he starts mocking Lee more openly for fun.
## Relationships
- [[Robert Orlando]] — father; Molotov was introduced to mafia life through him.
- [[Lee Mueller]] — Robert's lawyer; Molotov initially distrusts and mocks him, especially after learning Lee knows about Robert's mafia involvement.
