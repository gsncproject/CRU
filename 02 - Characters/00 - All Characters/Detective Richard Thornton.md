## Biography
Richard Thornton is a detective who killed [[Jamie Taylor]] after Jamie attempted to kill him. Richard then hid evidence of his involvement, leaving the public aware that Jamie had died but unaware of who killed him or the full circumstances.
His concealment becomes the central mystery of [[The Jamie Taylor Case - Overview|The Jamie Taylor Case]], in which [[Sam Taylor]], [[Winston Desjardins]], and [[Wren Carter]] investigate Jamie's death.
