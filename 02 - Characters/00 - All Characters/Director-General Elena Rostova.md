## Biography
Director-General Elena Rostova has been the Director-General of ARA for approximately twenty years. She is above the Washington Branch and therefore above [[Director Aiden Janson]], who directs the NYC Branch. She knows about [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]].
Elena would destroy MAC immediately if the decision were solely hers. Despite her strict reputation, she is fundamentally protective of people and believes ARA should serve that purpose.
