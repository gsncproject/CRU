## Biography
Mary is the mother of Sam Taylor and Jamie Taylor and the widow of James Taylor Sr. Jamie was her favorite child and the one who needed her most because of his neurodivergence. Jamie trusted her deeply, and she trusted him.

After James died, Mary focused more on Jamie because she knew Sam could handle herself. Following Jamie's death, Mary breaks down, blames herself, and isolates herself. Claire Madison begins taking care of her because she knows Sam cannot manage it alone.

Mary initially dismisses Sam's claims about Jamie giving her signs as a coping mechanism and thinks Sam is crazy.
