# Mary Taylor
- Full Name: Mary Taylor
- Birthday: July 26, 1978 — Newark, NJ
- Family: Mother of Sam Taylor and Jamie Taylor; widow of James Taylor Sr.
- Jamie was her favorite child and the one who needed her most because of his neurodivergence. He trusted her deeply, and she trusted him.
- After James died, Mary focused more on Jamie because she knew Sam could handle herself.
- After Jamie's death, Mary breaks down, blames herself, and isolates herself. Claire Madison begins taking care of her because she knows Sam cannot manage it alone.
- Mary initially dismisses Sam's claims about Jamie's signs as a coping mechanism and thinks Sam is crazy.
