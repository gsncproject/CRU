## Biography
Maurice Curtis founded MAC in 1947 with his best friend [[Friedrich Ziegel]]. MAC was originally created to expose government secrets from World War II and the post-war period. Through this work, MAC eventually discovered that the supernatural exists and began researching it.

Maurice is the father of [[John Curtis]], who later becomes one of MAC's two directors alongside [[Winston Zander]].
