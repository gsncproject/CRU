## Biography
Claire Madison is the wife of [[Étienne Desjardins]] and mother of [[Winston Desjardins]]. She called her son Winston throughout his childhood, contributing to his dislike of his full name and preference for the nickname Winn.
After [[Jamie Taylor]] dies, Claire begins taking care of [[Mary Taylor]], recognizing that [[Sam Taylor]] cannot handle everything on her own.
