## Biography
Winston Desjardins, commonly called **Winn**, is Sam Taylor's best friend. One week after the events of [[Clyde Rules - Overview|Clyde Rules]], he joins Sam and the new student Wren Carter to investigate the death of Sam's younger brother, [[Jamie Taylor]].
Winn is part of the central investigative group in [[The Jamie Taylor Case - Overview|The Jamie Taylor Case]].
