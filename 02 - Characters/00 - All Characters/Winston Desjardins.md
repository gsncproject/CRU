## Personality
Winn is close to Sam and is willing to believe her when strange things begin happening. He is more emotionally affected by Jamie's death than he lets show, but he and Jamie were not close. He hates the name Winston because his mother [[Claire Madison]] called him that throughout his life.
## Biography
Winston Desjardins, usually called Winn, is [[Sam Taylor]]'s best friend. They have known each other since middle school and became very close friends. During freshman year, Winn developed a crush on Sam.
One week after [[Clyde Rules - Overview|Clyde Rules]], Winn joins Sam and the new student [[Wren Carter]] in investigating Jamie's death. TJTC is told from Winn's POV. Winn believes Sam and eventually begins seeing [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]] himself.
Winn's relationship with his parents is complicated. His father [[Étienne Desjardins]] worries about their immigrant status and considers Winn too American despite him being born French. Winn already has a green card while Étienne does not. Claire is warm and caring, but Winn hates being called Winston.
