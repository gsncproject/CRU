## Biography
Howard Beckham was a Director of ARA. In 1988, during [[1988 Clyde Incident]], [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]] escaped from a clay box stored at a MAC blacksite during [[Director Aiden Janson]]'s first mission as an ARA recruit.

Clyde impersonated Beckham after his escape. When Janson returned to Washington, he encountered the real Beckham but believed him to be Clyde and killed him. Janson was subsequently detained and tried; he was ultimately found innocent after the circumstances of psychological torture were considered and later became ARA's new Director.
