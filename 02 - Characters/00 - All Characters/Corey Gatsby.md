# Corey Gatsby
## Basic Information
- Full Name: Corey Alexandra Gatsby
- Aliases: Agent Gatsby
- Birthday: July 7th 1997
- Residence: [[New York City, NY (CRU)]]
- Occupation: Resident Tech and Field Agent at the [[Regional Headquarters New York City]]
- Status: Deceased
## Personality
Corey is the calm, caring, and practical part of her group, often acting as the “mother” who keeps everyone together and stops situations from descending into chaos. She is friendly and empathic but also notably sassy. Around [[Kieran Jamie Shaw]] and [[Kyra Aldun]], she is comfortable enough to bicker and argue with them like family.
## Appearance
Corey has dark brown hair, usually tied back because she considers it practical, brown eyes, and glasses. Outside of work she often wears oversized hoodies; while working in the field she wears tactical armor.
## Biography
Corey works as both a resident tech and field agent at the [[Regional Headquarters New York City]]. She is part of the team working with [[Kieran Jamie Shaw]] and [[Kyra Aldun]]. The three quickly develop a close friendship and become a highly familiar, argumentative team.

During [[A Techies Guide to Trouble - Overview|A Techies Guide to Trouble]], Corey is part of the team tasked with investigating the vigilante interfering with [[Alien Research Agency|ARA]] operations in New York City. She regularly clashes with Jamie over technical problems while Kyra has to deal with their arguments. Corey also repeatedly reminds Jamie that he is not legally an adult yet.

Corey is connected to the wider ARA group and knows [[Hayden Shaw]], [[Sage Carter]], [[Oscar Zander]], [[Finn Kaelen]], and [[Felix Mercer]].
