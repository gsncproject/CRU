# Corey Gatsby  
## Basic Information 
- Full Name: Corey Alexandra Gatsby 
- Aliases: Agent Gatsby
- Birthday: July 7th 1997 
- Residence: [[New York City, NY (CRU)]]
- Occupation: Resident Tech and Field Agent at the [[Regional Headquarters New York City]]
- Status: Dead 
## Personality
Corey is the calm part of the group and serves mostly as the „mother“ keeping everything together and from exploding into chaos. She is also very caring but can also be very sassy.
## Appearance 
Corey has dark brown hair, usually tied back because „it‘s practical“, with brown eyes and glasses. Often she wears oversized hoodies when she is not wearing her tactical armor.
