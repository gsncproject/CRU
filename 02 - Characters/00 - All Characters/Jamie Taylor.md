# Jamie Taylor (CRU)

- Status: Deceased

## Personality
Jamie is a twelve-year-old who struggles with ADHD and dyslexia. He is shown as impulsive and increasingly reckless throughout [[Clyde Rules - Overview|Clyde Rules]]. His trust in authority figures deteriorates as his relationship with [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]] grows, eventually contributing to the events that lead to his death.

In [[The Circus of Your Mind - Overview|The Circus of Your Mind]], Jamie appears as a frightened young boy in [[Jeremy Cade]]'s dream-theater experiences. He is quiet and fidgety, plays with his hoodie sleeves, and asks Jeremy whether he ever gets scared of death. He appears particularly afraid of Clyde.

## Appearance
Jamie is described in [[The Circus of Your Mind - Overview|The Circus of Your Mind]] as a boy with blonde, curly hair who is no more than twelve years old. He wears a hoodie and repeatedly fidgets with its sleeves. His small stature and swinging legs emphasize his young age.

## Biography
Jamie is a twelve-year-old living in [[Northbridge, NY (CRU)]]. His story is primarily told in [[Clyde Rules - Overview|Clyde Rules]], where he begins encountering [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]]. Jamie increasingly trusts Clyde while becoming less trusting of authority figures. Clyde becomes the person Jamie trusts most, and Jamie's growing dependence on him leads to increasingly reckless behavior. The events of the story ultimately result in Jamie's death.

Jamie is also present in the dream-theater experiences of [[Jeremy Cade]] in [[The Circus of Your Mind - Overview|The Circus of Your Mind]]. Jeremy encounters Jamie as a frightened twelve-year-old boy sitting on the stage of an empty theater. Jamie recognizes Jeremy as a writer and asks him whether he is scared of death. He then warns Jeremy that Clyde is there. Jeremy later encounters Jamie and Clyde again, where Jamie runs off the stage while Clyde continues speaking to Jeremy.

The exact nature of Jamie's appearances in Jeremy's dreams, and whether Jeremy is seeing Jamie's actual spirit, a memory, a manifestation, or something else, is currently unknown.
