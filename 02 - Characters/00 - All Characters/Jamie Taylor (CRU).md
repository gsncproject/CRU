## Biography
Jamie Taylor is the younger brother of [[Sam Taylor (CRU)]] and the son of [[Mary Taylor]] and [[James Taylor Senior]]. His father died when Jamie was young, leaving him with a need for a fatherly figure. Clyde found Jamie and exploited that vulnerability, becoming a figure Jamie became deeply attached to and trusted.

Jamie eventually believed Clyde over everyone else. [[Detective Richard Thornton]] tried to stop Jamie after Jamie became involved with Clyde. Jamie attempted to kill Richard, but Richard killed Jamie in the process and then attempted to hide the evidence of what happened.

Jamie’s death is the central event investigated in [[The Jamie Taylor Case - Overview|The Jamie Taylor Case]].
