## Notes
- New York City is the main U.S. setting for the NYC branch of the [[Alien Research Agency]].
- [[Director Aiden Janson]] directs the NYC branch; the Washington branch headed by [[Director-General Elena Rostova]] is above it.
- [[Kyra Aldun]] is a field agent in the NYC branch and part of Lab Four.
- [[Kieran Jamie Shaw]] is connected to the NYC ARA as a tech worker and is the protagonist/narrator of [[A Techies Guide to Trouble - Overview|A Techies Guide to Trouble]].
- [[Corey Gatsby]] is one of the tech people associated with Lab Four in [[A Techies Guide to Trouble - Overview|A Techies Guide to Trouble]]. She is deceased by the time of [[Weight of Tomorrow - Overview|Weight of Tomorrow]].
- [[Sage Carter]] joins Lab Four in [[Weight of Tomorrow - Overview|Weight of Tomorrow]] and is secretly Janson's daughter.
- [[Malik Hart]] owns the [[Old Golden Inn]], which serves as a recurring meeting place for Kieran, Kyra, Corey, Hayden and Malik.
- [[Oscar Zander]] is Kieran's best friend and the vigilante ARA is trying to catch in [[A Techies Guide to Trouble - Overview|A Techies Guide to Trouble]].
