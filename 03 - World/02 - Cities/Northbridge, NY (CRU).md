## Notes
- Northbridge is the primary setting of [[Clyde Rules - Overview|Clyde Rules]] and the immediate setting of [[The Jamie Taylor Case - Overview|The Jamie Taylor Case]].
- [[Jamie Taylor]] lived in Northbridge with his mother [[Mary Taylor]] and older sister [[Sam Taylor]].
- [[Jamie Taylor]] attended [[Northbridge Middle School]].
- Jamie's death occurs at [[Old Sunders Bridge]] after an encounter with [[Detective Richard Thornton]].
- [[Winston Desjardins]], [[Wren Carter]] and [[Sam Taylor]] investigate Jamie's death in [[The Jamie Taylor Case - Overview|The Jamie Taylor Case]].
- [[Claire Madison]] and [[Étienne Desjardins]] are part of the local Desjardins family household.
