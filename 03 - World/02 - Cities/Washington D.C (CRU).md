## Notes
- Washington D.C. is the location of the global headquarters of the [[Alien Research Agency]].
- The Washington Branch of the ARA is above the NYC branch and is headed by [[Director-General Elena Rostova]].
- [[Director Aiden Janson]] lived in Washington D.C. before becoming Director of the NYC branch; [[Peter Janson]] is his uncle and raised him after Janson's parents died.
- The events of [[Origins - Overview|Origins]] connect Washington D.C. to the early history of [[Clyde Barrow (CRU)]].
- [[Director Howard Beckham]] was killed by Janson in connection with the 1988 Clyde incident after Clyde had impersonated Beckham.
- [[Maurice Curtis]] and [[John Curtis]] have historical ties to Washington D.C. and MAC.
