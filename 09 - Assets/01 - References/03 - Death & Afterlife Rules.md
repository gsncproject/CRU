# Death & Afterlife Rules

## 1. Death
- Death ends biological life, but it does not necessarily end consciousness. When a person dies, their body is gone, but their consciousness may continue to exist.
- A normal death can therefore lead to several possibilities: the consciousness may move on, remain on Earth as a ghost, or potentially enter whatever exists beyond Earth. The exact destination after death is not completely understood.

## 2. Souls
- Souls objectively exist in the CRU. This has been established through research connected to the [[Alien Research Agency]] and through inhuman experiments conducted by the [[Marble Arch Council]].
- The existence of souls is known, but their full nature is not understood. There is no established scientific explanation for everything a soul can do.

## 3. Ghosts
- Anyone can theoretically become a ghost.
- A ghost remains the person they were while alive. They retain their memories, personality, identity, emotions, and knowledge. Death does not automatically cause them to forget.
- Ghosts can continue learning and experiencing new things after death.
- Ghosts can physically interact with the world. They can be perceived by some living people while remaining completely imperceptible to others. Different people may also perceive the same ghost differently.
- A person who is not trapped by Clyde can choose to remain on Earth or move on.

## 4. The Afterlife
- The CRU does not currently establish one definitive explanation for what happens when a consciousness moves on. Possibilities include encountering God, Satan, or something else entirely.
- Characters and organizations may have beliefs about the afterlife, but the complete truth remains unknown.

## 5. Clyde's Death Trap
Clyde fundamentally changes what happens after death.

If a person dies because of Clyde, their consciousness becomes trapped on Earth. Escaping this state is extremely difficult and may be almost impossible.

Clyde does not directly kill people. He cannot simply kill someone himself. However, a death caused by circumstances involving Clyde can still result in the person's consciousness being trapped.

Clyde himself cannot be killed. Attempting to treat Clyde like an ordinary living being that can simply be killed is therefore the wrong approach.

## 6. Clyde and the Dead
Clyde can interact with ghosts and can enter dreams. Ghosts recognize that Clyde is not one of their own kind.

Clyde can impersonate both living and dead people. He can therefore appear in the form of someone who has died, including [[Jamie Taylor]].

An apparent encounter with a dead person is therefore not automatically proof that the actual person's consciousness is present.

## 7. Resurrection
Resurrection is possible in the CRU, but it is supernatural/paranormal rather than an ordinary scientific process.

A person who is resurrected is never completely themselves again. They may retain their memories, personality, identity, and recognizable characteristics, but something fundamental has changed.

The exact nature of that change is currently unknown.

Resurrection can also go wrong, meaning that returning from death does not guarantee a completely normal return to life.

## 8. Death Across the Multiverse
Alternate-universe versions of a person are separate individuals. If one version dies, it does not directly affect another version of that character in another universe.

A dead person or ghost can potentially cross into another universe. The existence or death of one version does not make the other versions the same person.

For example, alternate versions of the same character can have completely different lives, personalities, and fates while remaining distinct individuals.

## 9. Jamie Taylor
[[Jamie Taylor]]'s death demonstrates several of these rules.

After [[Jamie Taylor]] is killed by [[Detective Richard Thornton]], Jamie's consciousness continues to exist. Jamie knows that he is dead, but he is confused because he can still see other people while they cannot normally see him.

Jamie can sometimes communicate with his sister [[Sam Taylor]]. Sam interprets some of these encounters and signs as Jamie trying to help her solve his death.

Jamie can also appear in [[The Circus of Your Mind - Overview|The Circus of Your Mind]].

However, because Clyde can impersonate Jamie, Sam and other characters cannot automatically assume that every appearance of Jamie is actually Jamie.

## 10. The ARA
The [[Alien Research Agency]] knows that souls and ghosts exist and maintains files and research concerning supernatural phenomena.

The ARA can detect supernatural entities. This is one of the purposes of its research, although its investigations can be disrupted or redirected by the [[Marble Arch Council]].

The ARA does not currently know exactly what Clyde is, although it has suspicions that something unusual is involved.

## 11. Open Questions
- What exactly is a soul?
- What determines whether a person remains on Earth naturally rather than moving on?
- What exactly exists beyond Earth after a consciousness moves on?
- What fundamentally changes when a person is resurrected?
- How can resurrection be performed, and why does it sometimes go wrong?
- Why can Clyde trap the consciousnesses of people whose deaths are caused by him?
- Is there any reliable way to free a consciousness trapped by Clyde?

These questions are intentionally unresolved until established elsewhere in the CRU.
